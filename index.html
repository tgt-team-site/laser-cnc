What is a laser CNC?

CNC means Computer Numerical Control. A computer drives the machine, and a focused, high-power laser beam cuts, etches or engraves wood, acrylic, leather and soft metals.

Precise

Complex designs repeat exactly, with no human error.

Fast

Intricate jobs finish in minutes.

Efficient

Less scrap, lower cost per part.

How we built it

  1. Mechanical design

    The frame, motion mounts and casing were modeled in SolidWorks to guarantee fit and tolerances.

  2. Structural assembly

    We built the chassis and aligned the rails, drives and brackets for smooth travel.

  3. Electronics and firmware

    An Arduino runs GRBL firmware, turning G-code into exact stepper movements.

  4. Final integration

    Everything works together from file to finished cut.
